COACH

A tool designed to provide valuable insights into workspace utilization and environmental conditions. Leveraging the power of Internet of Things (IoT) technology, COACH captures, stores, and presents real-time data to enable informed decision-making.

It combines IoT technology with advanced sensors to collect data on occupancy, environmental factors, and more. The tool aims to provide a detailed understanding of workspace use and their associated environmental conditions.
